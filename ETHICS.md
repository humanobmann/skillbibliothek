# Ethics and scope

This repository contains agent skills for research, automation, design, analysis and operational workflows. These skills are intended for lawful, proportionate, transparent use.

## Core rules

- Use public, permitted data sources and documented workflows only.
- Respect platform terms, local law, privacy expectations and access boundaries.
- Do not use these skills to target private individuals for harassment, doxxing, stalking or non-consensual monitoring.
- Do not bypass access controls, authentication, paywalls, anti-bot protections or secured systems.
- Do not impersonate people, hide your identity to evade platform rules, or use deceptive practices to access restricted information.
- Treat sensitive and personal data with minimization, consent and secure handling.

## Preferred use

- public-interest research
- lawful due diligence and verification
- internal documentation and operational support
- reproducible, auditable AI workflows

## Out of scope

- covert surveillance
- credential theft or account takeover
- unauthorized access to private systems
- automated abuse, harassment or discriminatory targeting
- use of scraped or leaked data for non-consensual publication

## When in doubt

If a task requires deception, evasion, unauthorized access or non-public data, stop and re-scope the task before proceeding.
