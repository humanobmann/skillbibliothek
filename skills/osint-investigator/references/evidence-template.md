# Investigation output template

## Executive finding
State the most important conclusion in 2 to 5 sentences and give its confidence.

## Intelligence requirement
State what question was investigated, for what timeframe and jurisdiction.

## Findings
For each finding include:

- finding title;
- status: Confirmed, Likely, Possible, Disputed, Not found;
- factual observation;
- source or record;
- what the evidence proves;
- what it does not prove;
- counter-evidence or alternative explanation;
- confidence and reason.

## Timeline
Use date, event, actor, source, confidence.

## Relationship or ownership map
Use source, entity A, relationship type, entity B, effective date or period, evidence, confidence.

## Evidence register
Use source ID, publisher, title, URL or record ID, publication date, access date, source tier, claim supported, archive or preserved copy, notes.

## Contradictions and gaps
List unresolved conflicts, unavailable records, paywalled sources not accessed, identity ambiguities, and jurisdictions not checked.

## Safe-to-publish claim set
List only claims supported strongly enough for publication. Attribute allegations and unresolved proceedings explicitly.

## Next verification steps
List the smallest number of concrete checks that would most increase confidence.
