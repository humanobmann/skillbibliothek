# Source protection and data handling

- Minimise collection and access. Keep source identity, contact channel and any authentication material outside ordinary case notes and AI prompts.
- Do not upload raw documents with hidden metadata or sensitive personal data to third-party services until an authorised risk review approves it.
- Preserve originals, but create a redacted working copy for analysis and sharing. Record transformations so a fact can be traced to the original.
- Use an agreed secure channel and access-controlled storage. Do not promise absolute anonymity, security or legal privilege.
- Consider whether contact, publication, a visible profile view, a query log, cross-border transfer or retention could expose a source, subject or reporter. Document mitigations.
- Retain only what is necessary for the stated story and complaint/correction process; set a review/deletion date. Never retain credentials, passwords or copied leak corpora.
