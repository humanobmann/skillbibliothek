# International standards and legal-research baseline

This is a working research baseline, not legal advice and not a claim that one jurisdiction's journalism exemption applies in another.

- **ICCPR Article 19:** protects the freedom to seek, receive and impart information across frontiers, subject to lawful and necessary restrictions. Article 17 protects against arbitrary or unlawful privacy interference. Start with both rights, not one of them.
- **ECHR Article 10:** protects expression and information; Article 8 protects private and family life. European reporting needs a contextual balancing analysis, including contribution to a debate of public interest, notoriety/role, conduct, method of obtaining the information, content/form/consequences and procedural fairness.
- **Council of Europe CM/Rec(2016)4:** recognises the democratic value of journalism, source and material protection, and journalist safety. It does not licence intrusion into private systems or unsafe publication.
- **GDPR Article 85:** requires Member States to reconcile data protection with freedom of expression and information, including journalism. The necessary exemptions/derogations are implemented through national law and are purpose- and necessity-bound.
- **Cross-border work:** add the local data-protection, media, defamation, secrecy, computer-misuse, copyright and court-reporting rules that apply where collection, storage, target and publication occur. Escalate material conflict or uncertainty to a qualified reviewer.

Primary sources to consult at the time of the case:

- United Nations, [International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights](https://2covenants.ohchr.org/About-ICCPR.html)
- Council of Europe, [Recommendation CM/Rec(2016)4](https://rm.coe.int/recommendation-cm-rec-2016-4-of-the-committee-of-ministers-to-member-s/1680983254)
- EUR-Lex, [GDPR Article 85](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

Do not infer that material is lawful merely because it is public, free, indexed, archived, available across a border or useful to a story.
