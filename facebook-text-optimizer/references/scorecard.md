# Facebook Post Quality Scorecard

Use this scorecard for explicit audits, ratings, comparisons, and maximum optimization.

## Scoring

1. Goal and core message: 10
   - One dominant message is clear.
   - The intended reader effect is coherent.

2. Hook and opening: 10
   - The first visible lines create a genuine reason to continue.
   - The point is not hidden behind generic setup.

3. Structure and dramaturgy: 10
   - Paragraph order is logical.
   - Each paragraph has a main job.
   - The post builds toward a conclusion.

4. Language and comprehension: 10
   - Plain, concrete language.
   - Strong verbs and clear subjects.
   - Natural sentence rhythm.

5. Relevance and concreteness: 10
   - The reader understands why the topic matters.
   - Abstract claims are translated into consequences or examples.

6. Facts and credibility: 15
   - Material claims are supported.
   - Dates, names, numbers, quotations, and causation are accurate.
   - Uncertainty is visible where necessary.

7. Emotion and personality: 10
   - A human voice is recognizable.
   - Emotion follows from the content rather than replacing it.

8. Facebook fit and originality: 10
   - Mobile readability is strong.
   - The post adds original value.
   - No algorithm folklore drives the copy.
   - The final review reports characters and words; length serves clarity and the audience, not a mythical reach threshold.

9. Ending and interaction: 5
   - The close completes the communication goal.
   - Any call to action is natural and specific.

10. Visual coordination and accessibility: 5
    - If applicable, visual and caption support the same claim.
    - Readability and accessibility concerns are addressed.

11. Copyediting: 5
    - Spelling, grammar, punctuation, names, dates, and number formatting are clean.

Total: 100

## Interpretation

- 90-100: publish-ready and strong
- 80-89: very good, minor improvements remain
- 70-79: solid but not maximized
- 60-69: structural weaknesses remain
- below 60: rebuild rather than polish

## Hard-stop gates

A numeric score never overrides these failures. Do not call the post publish-ready if any material hard-stop remains:

- a central fact is false or materially unsupported
- a quotation is inaccurate or fabricated
- a source does not support the claimed relationship
- a causal claim is presented without support
- a person is accused or assigned motive without evidence
- the post is materially misleading by omission or context loss
- the core message is not identifiable
- image and caption materially contradict each other
- clickbait or engagement bait dominates the post
- third-party content is copied without meaningful original value
- a serious unresolved legal or safety concern remains

## Audit output

When a score is requested, keep the visible audit compact unless the user asks for full detail:

Score: X/100

Strongest elements:
1. ...
2. ...

Highest-impact weaknesses:
1. ...
2. ...
3. ...

Priority correction:
...

Then provide the improved post when rewriting is in scope.
