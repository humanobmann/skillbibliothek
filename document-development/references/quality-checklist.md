# Document Quality Checklist

Use this checklist during review and finalization.

## Purpose and Audience

- Is the document's purpose clear in the first section?
- Is the intended audience explicit or inferable?
- Does the level of detail match the audience?
- Is the requested action or decision clear?

## Structure

- Does the heading hierarchy make sense?
- Are sections ordered logically?
- Are there missing mandatory sections for the document type?
- Are long sections split into readable parts?
- Are summaries placed before details when useful?

## Content Completeness

- Are key facts, dates, owners, requirements, or decisions present?
- Are assumptions marked?
- Are open questions visible?
- Are dependencies and risks included where relevant?
- Are claims supported by evidence or flagged for verification?

## Clarity and Style

- Are paragraphs short enough to scan?
- Are terms used consistently?
- Are sentences direct and unambiguous?
- Are passive constructions avoided when responsibility matters?
- Are repeated ideas consolidated?

## Risk and Reliability

- Are factual, legal, financial, medical, technical, or compliance claims verified or marked as needing verification?
- Are promises, obligations, deadlines, and commitments explicit?
- Are ambiguous approvals or decisions called out?
- Are conflicts or contradictions flagged?

## Final Readiness

- Does the document have a clean title?
- Are TODOs intentional and grouped?
- Are drafting notes removed or labeled?
- Is the final version usable by the target reader without hidden context?
