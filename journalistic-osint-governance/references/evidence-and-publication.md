# Evidence, fairness and publication ledger

For every material claim, retain a minimal internal record:

| Field | Record |
|---|---|
| Claim | Exact proposition; label fact, allegation, inference or unresolved lead. |
| Source | Publisher/record holder, URL or identifier, access date, original publication date and preservation reference. |
| Provenance | Primary/secondary, original collector where known and independence from other sources. |
| Grade | Source reliability and information confidence; contradictions and identity-collision checks. |
| Necessity | Why the item is needed for the story and the least-identifying form suitable for publication. |
| Response | Who was offered a right of reply, what was put, deadline, answer/no answer and reason if no approach. |
| Decision | Verify, qualify, omit, hold, publish or correct; responsible editor and date. |

Publication prose must say what the evidence supports—not what a reader may infer. Attribute allegations, preserve material denials where relevant, use precise dates, and do not turn absence of evidence into evidence of absence. A correction path and source-protection review belong in the final publication plan.
