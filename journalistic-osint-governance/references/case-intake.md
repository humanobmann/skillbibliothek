# Case intake and scope record

Complete before collection. Use concise language; never enter raw credentials, private source contact details or unnecessary special-category data.

| Field | Required record |
|---|---|
| Story question | A falsifiable question and the public decision/debate it informs. |
| Public-interest rationale | Why publication benefits a legitimate public debate; why less intrusive reporting cannot answer it. |
| Subject and discriminators | Exact entity, collision checks and people explicitly out of scope. |
| Jurisdictions | Collection, subject, storage, publication and audience countries; local-law questions to review. |
| Authority | Commission/editor, documented mandate or lawful self/organisation security authority. |
| In bounds | Named sources, selectors, passive methods and time window. |
| Out of bounds | Accounts, private groups, interaction, scanning, credentials, family/minors, sensitive data and prohibited techniques. |
| Risk and harm | Privacy, safety, retaliation, defamation, source, data-security and cross-border risks. |
| Retention | Minimum case material, access owner, review date and deletion trigger. |
| Approval | Editor; legal/safeguarding reviewer where triggered; date and decision. |
| Stop conditions | Scope expansion, identity ambiguity, sensitive-data discovery, imminent harm, or unresolved legal question. |

`HOLD FOR REVIEW` is the default when any required field is missing.
