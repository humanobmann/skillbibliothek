# Source priorities

## Tier 1: Primary authoritative
Laws, official registers, court documents, regulator decisions, parliamentary records, public procurement records, company filings, audited reports, official datasets, original speeches or documents.

## Tier 2: High-quality independent
Established investigative journalism, reputable news agencies, peer-reviewed literature, recognised research institutions, specialist publications with transparent sourcing.

## Tier 3: Discovery and aggregation
Commercial databases, people/company aggregators, search snippets, indexes, cached summaries. Use as leads and trace to originals.

## Tier 4: Weak or volatile
Anonymous posts, screenshots without provenance, reposts, forums, unattributed graphics, AI summaries, unverified social claims. Never use alone for material conclusions.

## Corroboration rule
Two articles that copy the same wire story are one source chain. Two sites repeating the same press release are not independent corroboration. Prefer independent underlying records.
