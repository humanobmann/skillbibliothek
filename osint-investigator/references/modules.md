# Collection modules

Use only modules needed for the intelligence requirement.

## A. Baseline web and news
Search current web results, official pages, news archives, press releases, PDFs, and cached or archived versions. Record exact queries and publication dates.

## B. Public statements and social media
Collect only publicly visible posts or statements relevant to the public-interest question. Preserve context, date, account identity, edits when visible, and original links. Avoid private-account workarounds or follower scraping.

## C. Public records and institutions
Check laws, parliamentary material, official gazettes, company registers, procurement records, lobbying or transparency registers, regulators, sanctions lists, court records where lawfully public, nonprofit records, public funding, and audit reports.

## D. Ownership and corporate networks
Resolve exact legal entities first. Map registration numbers, parent/subsidiary links, directors or officers when public and relevant, beneficial ownership only where lawfully public, acquisitions, shareholdings, foundation or trust structures, related-party transactions, and public contracts. Distinguish control from minority interest and historical from current ownership.

## E. Money and procurement trails
Trace public grants, subsidies, procurement awards, campaign finance where public, annual reports, disclosed related-party transactions, state aid, EU funding, lobbying disclosures, and public budgets. Do not infer corruption from a payment or relationship alone.

## F. Litigation, regulation, sanctions, PEP and adverse media
Use original court or regulator records first. Record procedural posture, date, jurisdiction, case number, disposition, appeal status, and whether allegations were proven, dismissed, settled, or unresolved. Sanctions and PEP screening must name the lists actually checked.

## G. Archives and changed content
Use lawful archives such as official archives or public web archives to compare versions. Record capture dates. A deleted or changed page proves change, not motive.

## H. Documents and metadata
Inspect user-supplied or lawfully downloaded public documents for authorship fields, timestamps, revisions, embedded references, document properties, and inconsistencies. Metadata can be wrong or stripped and must be corroborated.

## I. Image, video and event verification
Check reverse-image results, original upload chronology, visual landmarks, weather/time clues where appropriate, metadata if available, and independent coverage. Never authenticate solely from visual model confidence.

## J. Domains and public web infrastructure
Use passive public information such as DNS, certificate transparency, public WHOIS/RDAP, historical DNS, technology fingerprints, and attributable hosting information for ownership or provenance questions. No scanning, exploitation, or authentication testing.

## K. Relationship and network mapping
Create sourced edges only. Classify edge type and date. Shared attendance, follows, or photographs alone do not prove coordination, financing, control, or ideological alignment.

## L. International and cross-border records
Resolve names, transliterations, jurisdictions, local register rules, and historical entity changes. Search in local language where feasible. Document coverage limitations.

## M. Claim and disinformation verification
Trace a claim to its earliest available source, distinguish original evidence from repetition, check context, date, image reuse, selective quotation, and credible counter-evidence.

## N. Public-ledger financial traces
For public blockchains, analyse addresses and transactions only as public ledger data. An address is not a person without independent attribution evidence. Do not facilitate theft, laundering, evasion, or deanonymisation of private individuals.

## O. Transport and asset records
Use public asset or transport records only for organisations, public-interest assets, or clearly legitimate research. Do not track private individuals' real-time movements or produce stalking-enabling location patterns.
